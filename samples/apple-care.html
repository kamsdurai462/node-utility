      AppleCare+ Benefits

      Complete Device Protection

      Enjoy unlimited accidental damage protection from drops, spills, and cracked screens. AppleCare+ also extends your hardware warranty, covers battery replacements, and protects accessories purchased with your device.

      Global Coverage & Expert Access

      Get priority access to Apple experts for troubleshooting and support. With worldwide service options, you can get repairs at any Apple Store or authorized service provider, ensuring you're protected wherever you go.

      Convenience and Peace of Mind

      Eliminate the stress and cost of unexpected repairs. AppleCare+ simplifies the entire process with Apple's trusted service network, giving you total peace of mind.

      Frequently Asked Questions

      You can start an AppleCare+ plan when buying a new product in an etisalat retail store, on the etisalat website, or through the etisalat UAE app, or at any point within 60 days of the original purchase date.
      For 60 days after a new Apple purchase, you will need to enter your device's IMEI number to authenticate and complete the transaction.

      Coverage begins immediately at the time of purchase. Your device is covered as long as you purchase the plan within 60 days of the device purchase.

      You can get support for your hardware and software needs by contacting an Apple expert via chat, call, or the Apple Support app. You can also visit an Apple Store or an Apple Authorized Service Provider.

      When you protect your iPad with AppleCare+, you’ll get coverage for Accidental Damage for one Apple Pencil and one Apple‑branded iPad keyboard. AppleCare+ also covers any in-box cables and power adapters that came with your covered Apple product.

      AppleCare+ offers unlimited incidents of accidental damage protection. This includes coverage for accidents like drops, spills, and liquid damage.

      It depends on your product and the type of repair. Same-day service is available for iPhone in most major metropolitan areas. For some products, we also offer Express Replacement Service, where we will send you a new device so you don’t have to wait for a repair.

      AppleCare+ offers quick and convenient repair options with low service fees.

      • There is a service fee of AED 99 for screen or back glass damage.
      • There is a service fee of AED 349 for other accidental damage.
      • There is no additional fee for a battery replacement if your battery holds less than 80% of its original capacity.
